CERTIFIED RESOLUTION BY MEMBERS
ABC COIT FURNITURE, LLC

Israel Kamionka, Kalmon Kamionka and Benjamin Tyberg (the “Members”), the only members
of ABC Coit Furniture, LLC (the “LLC”), consent and agree that the following company resolution
was made on the day of March 2026.

We hereby unanimously consent to the adoption of this resolution as if it was adopted at a specially
called meeting of the members of this LLC. We certify that this resolution is in conformity with
the Certificate of Formation of the LLC and that same is in full force and effect.

In accordance with State laws and the Certificate of Formation, as amended, the members have
unanimously decided to purchase all of the outstanding and issued shares of Arnold Reception
Desks, Inc. (*“ARD”).

Now, therefore, it is resolved, that the Members of the LLC, on behalf of the LLC, be and are
hereby authorized to:

(a) enter into the Stock Purchase Agreement for the purchase of all of the outstanding
and issued share of ARD;

(b) execute, affirm, or sign any documents required in connection with the aforesaid
stock purchase transaction; and

(d) perform any acts to carry out this Resolution.

The members of the LLC hereby certify to ARD, the Seller, that this Resolution has been duly
adapted and that it is in conformity with the Certificate of Formation of the LLC.

We, the undersigned members of ABC Coit Furniture, LLC, unanimously consent and agree to all
of the above on this @ day of March 2026.
