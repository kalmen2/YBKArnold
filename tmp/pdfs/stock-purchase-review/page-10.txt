ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Asset Purchase Agreement (as amended, modified or supplemented from time to time,
this “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of March 1, 2026, by and between YBK Arnold
LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company with its registered office at 120 Coit Street, Irvington,
NJ 07111 (the “Buyer”), and Arnold Kolax Furniture, Inc., a New Jersey corporation with its
principal place of business at 120 Coit Street, Irvington, NJ 07111 (the “Seller” and collectively
with Buyer, the “Parties”).

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Arnold Kolax Furntirue, Inc. (“AKF”) is in the business of manufacturing
custom made furniture (the “Business”);

WHEREAS, AKF leases at 120 Coit Street, Irvington, NJ 07111 (the “Leased Premises”)
from YB Coit, LLC pursuant to a lease dated December 12, 2024 (the “Lease”);

WHEREAS, the Leased Premises suffered damage from a fire and the Seller submitted a
claim under its insurance policy for damages. The Seller is expecting future payments on the
insurance claim (the “Insurance Proceeds”);

WHEREAS, YB Coit, LLC commenced an action on the Lease to evict AFK in the
Superior Court of New Jersey for allegedly past due rent (the “Eviction Action”) and the Eviction
Action is pending under docket no. ESX-LT-019465-25;

WHEREAS, to resolve the Eviction Action and rent due, YB Coit, LLC has offered to
purchase the assets of AKF and the stock of Arnold Reception Desks, Inc.through YBK Amold,
LLC and ABC Coit Furniture, LLC,new entities formed by YB Coit LLC;

WHEREAS, the Buyer desires to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller desires to sell to
the Buyer, certain of the Seller’s assets related to the Business, and the Buyer desires to assume
from the Seller certain specified liabilities, in each case pursuant to the terms and subject to the
conditions set forth herein;

WHEREAS, subject to the terms and conditions contained herein, the Buyer shall
purchase and acquire from the Seller, the Purchased Assets (defined infra), and the Buyer shall
assume from the Seller the Assumed Liabilities (defined infra), all as more specifically provided
herein (the “Transactions”).
