CERTIFIED RESOLUTION BY MEMBERS
YBK ARNOLD, LLC

Israel Kamionka, Kalmon Kamionka and Benjamin Tyberg (the “Members”), the only members
of YBK Amold, LLC (the “LLC”), consent and agree that the following company resolution was
made on the day of March 2026.

We hereby unanimously consent to the adoption of this resolution as if it was adopted at a specially
called meeting of the members of this LLC. We certify that this resolution is in conformity with
the Certificate of Formation of the LLC and that same is in full force and effect.

In accordance with State laws and the Certificate of Formation, as amended, the members have
unanimously decided to purchase certain assets and assume certain liabilities of Arnold Kolax
Furniture, Inc. (AKF”) pursuant to an asset purchase agreement (the “APA”).

Now, therefore, it is resolved, that the Members of the LLC, on behalf of the LLC, be and are
hereby authorized to:

(a) enter into the APA for the purchase of certain assets and assumption of certain
liabilities of AKF as set forth in the APA;

(b) execute, affirm, or sign any documents required in connection with the aforesaid
asset purchase transaction;

(c) incur debt obligations to AKF as set forth in the APA and Note (as defined in the
APA) executed pursuant thereto;

(d) purchase certain assets of AKF and assume certain liabilities of AKF as set forth in
the APA;

(e) perform all acts required under the APA and execute and deliver any documents
required to consummate the asset purchase transaction set forth in the APA;

(d) perform any acts to carry out this Resolution.

The members of the LLC hereby certify to AKF, the Seller, that this Resolution has been duly
adapted and that it is in conformity with the Certificate of Formation of the LLC.

We, the undersigned members of YBK Armold, LLC, unanimously consent and agree to all of the
above on this 6 day of March 2026.

——

Kalmon Kamionka
Member YBK Arnold, LLC
