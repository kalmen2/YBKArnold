EXHIBIT "B" —- COMMISSION BALANCE ON OLD ORDERS

REPRESENTATIVE COMMISSION REPAYMENT
Initial Payment: The Representative will be paid $50,000 immediately upon contract agreement.

Subsequent Installment Payments: The Representative will be paid $5,000 on the 30th of each month for a
period of 3 months, starting on 5/30/26 and concluding on 7/30/26.

Commission Payment Schedule:

The total outstanding Commission Balance referenced in Schedule B is $177,457.92 ("Commission Balance"),
which is comprised of two portions:

Portion 1 — $140,615.07: This portion shall be repaid through the initial payment, the monthly installment
payments, and the additional percentage commissions described herein, until paid in full.

Portion 2 — $36,842.85: This portion represents commissions attributable to orders for which The Company has
not yet received client payment as of the Effective Date. As client payments are received on these orders, The
Company shall pay The Representative 12% of each received payment (on Net Sale Amount, exclusive of freight,
taxes, and installation), and such amounts shall be credited toward this portion until fully satisfied.

* — If the Company receives payment from the Client for any order listed on Schedule B, the Company agrees
to pay the Representative 12% of the received payment amount (for the Net Sale Amount, exclusive of
freight, taxes and installation).

* For all new orders, the Company shall pay the Representative a total of twenty percent (20%) of the Net
Sale Amount, of which ten percent (10%) constitutes the Representative’s earned commission and the
remaining ten percent (10%) shall be credited directly toward the Commission Balance, until such balance
is fully satisfied.

* The Company and the Representative will update the Commission Balance spreadsheet (Schedule B)
monthly. cl will require written approval.
